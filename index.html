OLAR / EurekAlert 12 AUG
Pacific Annular Warming Adds ~1.0 °C to 2026/27 El Niño Peak — Rare Triple-Region SST Pattern Could Push Event Past the 1997–98 All-Time Record

New research published in OLAR (Open-Access Life and Environmental Research, a Science Partner Journal) identifies a rare "Pacific Annular Warming" (PAW) pattern active in spring 2026: simultaneous positive SST anomalies in the western, northeastern, and southeastern Pacific form a ring around the central basin. Modelling experiments show that, without the PAW signal, the 2026/27 El Niño would peak as a moderate event (~1.9 °C Niño 3.4). The annular warming contributes an additional ~1.0 °C to the peak, placing the event squarely in super El Niño range. The result aligns closely with NOAA's 69% record-exceedance probability and the WMO ASO update to +2.9 °C.

OLAR (12 августа): «тихоокеанское кольцевое потепление» (PAW) весны 2026 г. — одновременные положительные аномалии ТПО на западе, СВ и ЮВ Тихого океана. Без PAW: умеренный Эль-Ниньо (~1,9 °C Niño 3.4). Вклад PAW: +1,0 °C к пику → «супер Эль-Ниньо». Согласуется с NOAA (69% вероятность рекорда) и ВМО ASO (+2,9 °C).

The settlement follows years of permitting delays, cost inflation, and supply chain disruptions that made the projects economically unviable. It is the largest offshore wind lease cancellation payment in US history and is expected to have a chilling effect on new lease sales. RWE retains its European, Australian, and Japanese offshore wind portfolios.

Met Office and Alan Turing Institute Release FastNet — Physics-Constrained GNN Matches or Exceeds Global Model; Sharper Frontal Structure Than Earlier Data-Driven Models

Met Office / Alan Turing Institute · July 2026

The Met Office and Alan Turing Institute published FastNet (July 13, 2026), a graph neural network (GNN) weather prediction model that matches or exceeds the performance of the operational Global Model on several standard forecast skill metrics. The key design innovation is physics-constrained training — loss terms that penalise physically unrealistic solutions — which reduces the blurring of fronts and thermal gradients that characterises earlier purely data-driven models. Evaluation on historical cases including Hurricane Ian (2022) and Storm Ciarán (2023) showed more realistic storm core structures and sharper wind gradient fields. The model runs at 0.25° resolution with 6-hourly output and is available under an open research licence.

Met Office + ATI (13 июля): FastNet — GNN-модель прогнозирования погоды с физически ограниченным обучением. По ряду метрик соответствует или превосходит операционный Global Model. Физические штрафные функции устраняют размытие фронтов и термических градиентов — главный недостаток ранних data-driven-моделей. Верификация на урагане Иан и шторме Ciarán: более реалистичные структуры ядра и резкие ветровые градиенты. Разрешение 0,25°, открытая лицензия.

HRRRCast: NOAA's AI Convection-Allowing Regional Model at 6-km Resolution — ResNet+GNN Architecture; 100× Faster Than HRRR; Outperforms HRRR on 20-dBZ Composite Reflectivity

Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems · AMS · Vol. 5, Issue 2 · 2026

Published in AMS Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems (Vol. 5, Issue 2, 2026), HRRRCast is NOAA's AI-based convection-allowing regional forecast model operating at 6-km horizontal resolution over the CONUS. A hybrid ResNet–GNN architecture is trained on three years of HRRR reanalysis and operational data. Probabilistic output is generated via denoising diffusion implicit models (DDIM), providing ensemble-equivalent spread at 1/100th the computational cost of full HRRR runs. HRRRCast outperforms HRRR on 20-dBZ composite reflectivity (severe convection) and matches HRRR on surface temperature and wind speed. HRRR V3 was publicly released in March 2026.

AMS AIES (том 5, вып. 2, 2026): HRRRCast — ИИ-модель NOAA на разрешении 6 км (CONUS). ResNet+GNN; вероятностный выход через DDIM (аналог ансамбля) — в 100 раз быстрее операционного HRRR. Превосходит HRRR по составной радарной отражаемости 20 дБЗ (сильная конвекция). Соответствует HRRR по T2m и скорости ветра. Публичный релиз V3: март 2026 г.

IJCAI 2026 Survey: "Deep Learning and Foundation Models for Weather Prediction" — Taxonomy of 200+ Architectures; Zero-Shot Generalisation, Extreme-Event Calibration, and Interpretability Identified as Key Frontiers

Ji et al. · IJCAI 2026 · accepted April 2026

Ji et al. present a comprehensive survey (accepted to IJCAI 2026) covering more than 200 deep learning and foundation model architectures applied to weather prediction. The taxonomy distinguishes deterministic models (GraphCast, Pangu-Weather, FourCastNet), probabilistic models (GenCast, EarthCast, SEEDS), and pre-trained foundation models with downstream fine-tuning (ClimaX, Aurora, FengWu-GHR). The survey identifies three open frontiers: (1) zero-shot generalisation across climate zones and data-sparse regions, (2) reliable extreme-event tail calibration, and (3) forecast explanation for operational meteorologists. A curated model registry and benchmark datasets are maintained at the linked GitHub repository.

Ji et al., IJCAI 2026 (принята апрель 2026): обзор 200+ ИИ-архитектур для прогнозирования погоды. Таксономия: детерминированные (GraphCast, Pangu, FourCastNet), вероятностные (GenCast, EarthCast, SEEDS) и предобученные модели (ClimaX, Aurora, FengWu-GHR). Открытые рубежи: нулевой перенос между климатическими зонами, калибровка хвостов экстремумов, интерпретируемость для оперативных метеорологов. Реестр моделей и бенчмарки — на GitHub.

AIES / AMS 10 AUG
AI Weather Models for Severe Convective Weather Forecasting — Seven-Model Evaluation; FourCastNet and Pangu-Weather Excel on Tornado Outbreak Environments; All Models Underestimate CAPE Tails

Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems · AMS · Vol. 5, Issue 1 · 2026

Published in AMS Artificial Intelligence for the Earth Systems (Vol. 5, Issue 1, 2026), this paper evaluates seven AI weather prediction models — including Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, FourCastNet, and AIFS — on their ability to predict severe convective weather environments at 24–48h lead times using a 2020–2024 severe weather events dataset. FourCastNet and Pangu-Weather showed the strongest performance on 500-hPa trough positioning and CAPE/CIN diagnostics relevant to tornado outbreak environments. Key identified gap: all models systematically underestimate convective available potential energy (CAPE) tails above the 95th percentile — a calibration deficit directly relevant to high-end severe weather events.

AMS AIES (том 5, вып. 1, 2026): оценка 7 AIWP-моделей (Pangu, GraphCast, FourCastNet, AIFS и др.) для прогноза конвективных условий на 24–48 ч (датасет опасных явлений 2020–2024). Лучший результат: FourCastNet и Pangu — ложбины 500 гПа, CAPE/CIN для торнадо. Ключевой недостаток: все модели недооценивают хвосты CAPE выше 95-го перцентиля — критично для высокоударных событий.

ESSOAr Preprint: All Four Tested AI Models Predicted the August 2026 European Heatwave's Northeast Shift Five Days in Advance — AIFS, Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, FourCastNet vs. IFS

ESSOAr preprint · ECMWF et al. · August 2026

A preprint posted to ESSOAr (August 12) by ECMWF and partner institutions provides the first multi-model real-time verification of four operational AI weather prediction systems — AIFS, Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, and FourCastNet — versus the operational IFS during the August 2026 European heatwave. All four AI models correctly predicted the heatwave's northeast shift from western France to Germany and the Balkans at least five days in advance. IFS showed marginally sharper temperature gradient placement; Pangu-Weather produced the best ensemble spread calibration during the rapid intensification phase (August 12–14). This constitutes one of the first systematically documented comparisons of multiple operational AIWP systems on a high-impact real-world event.

ESSOAr (12 августа): ECMWF et al. — первое систематическое сравнение AIFS, Pangu-Weather, GraphCast, FourCastNet vs. IFS на жаре 2026 г. Все 4 AIWP-системы верно предсказали НВ-смещение жары (Франция → Германия + Балканы) за ≥5 суток. IFS — чуть более чёткие температурные градиенты; Pangu — лучший разброс ансамбля в фазе быстрого усиления (12–14 августа). Одно из первых многомодельных верификационных исследований в режиме реального времени.
