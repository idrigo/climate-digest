Limnology & Oceanography / Scripps 19 JUL
Aquatic Deoxygenation Should Become the 10th Planetary Boundary, Scripps Study Argues

Ferrer et al. · Limnology and Oceanography · DOI: 10.1002/lno.70434 · Scripps / UC San Diego · 2026

A review paper in Limnology and Oceanography led by Erica Ferrer (Scripps Institution of Oceanography, UC San Diego) argues that the accelerating loss of dissolved oxygen from oceans, coastal waters, rivers, and lakes satisfies the criteria for a tenth planetary boundary. Current oxygen depletion trajectories across multiple ocean basins are already in the framework's "unsafe" zone, with cascading consequences including weakening of the biological carbon pump (reducing ocean CO₂ uptake), expansion of hypoxic dead zones, fishery collapse, and amplified nitrogen cycling feedbacks. Deep-basin oxygen recovery spans centuries to millennia. The authors call for treating aquatic oxygen loss with the same policy urgency as atmospheric CO₂ or biodiversity loss.

Обзор Ferrer et al. (Scripps/UCSD) в Limnology and Oceanography: потеря растворённого O₂ из океанов, рек и озёр соответствует критериям десятой планетарной границы — уже в «опасной» зоне. Последствия: ослабление биологической углеродной помпы, расширение мёртвых зон, обрушение рыбных запасов. Восстановление занимает века–тысячелетия.

arXiv / npj AI (ECMWF) 21 JUL
Multivariate Scoring Rules Match or Beat CRPS for Probabilistic NWP Training, ECMWF Study Finds

ECMWF team · arXiv:2607.19161 · physics.ao-ph / cs.LG · also published in npj Artificial Intelligence · July 21, 2026

Researchers at ECMWF systematically compare versions of the AIFS-CRPS global probabilistic weather model trained on different univariate and multivariate scoring rules: the (nearly) fair CRPS, a fair global energy score, and a graph energy score based on local grid-node neighbourhood structure. In the extratropics all three objectives produce similar forecast skill; in the tropics the graph energy score outperforms CRPS while the global energy score shows some degradation — suggesting multivariate scoring rules are a viable and potentially superior alternative to CRPS-based training for global ML weather forecasting. The AIFS-CRPS architecture paper has concurrently been peer-reviewed in npj Artificial Intelligence, formalising the model's status alongside its operational deployment at ECMWF.

Команда ECMWF сравнила AIFS-CRPS, обученный на трёх scoring rules: почти честный CRPS, честный глобальный energy score и graph energy score (структура соседства сетки). В экстратропиках навык сходен; в тропиках graph energy score лучше, глобальный — незначительно хуже. Вывод: многомерные функции потерь — жизнеспособная альтернатива CRPS для глобального ML-прогнозирования. AIFS-CRPS опубликован в npj Artificial Intelligence.

arXiv (UC San Diego) 06 JUL
On the Genealogy of Machine Learning Weather Prediction: How NWP's Initial-Value-Problem Frame Constrains ML Architecture

Mohammad Hassan Erfani · UC San Diego · arXiv:2607.05045 · physics.ao-ph · July 6, 2026

Mohammad Hassan Erfani (UC San Diego) surveys how modern machine-learning weather prediction inherited the initial-value-problem framing of numerical weather prediction — treating forecasting as a learned autoregressive time-stepping problem mapping an initial state forward in time — and argues this inheritance implicitly constrains architecture selection while limiting exploration of alternative paradigms. The paper distinguishes two traditions: "scientific surrogate modeling," where ML respects the physical constraints of the NWP system, and "free-form data-driven modeling," where atmospheric fields are treated as spatiotemporal sequences without explicit physical constraints. The analysis argues that current MLWP sits between the two but leans so heavily on the IVP frame that genuinely data-native architectures remain underexplored — a framing the author suggests will become important as the field reaches the limits of imitation-of-physics approaches.
