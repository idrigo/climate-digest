GRL / Carbon Brief 09 MAR
GRL: Global Warming Rate Has Nearly Doubled Since 2015 — Foster & Rahmstorf Find Background Rate of ~0.35°C/Decade After Removing ENSO, Volcanic, and Solar Signals

Foster G. & Rahmstorf S. · Geophysical Research Letters · 2026 · DOI:10.1029/2025GL118804

A study by Grant Foster and Stefan Rahmstorf published in Geophysical Research Letters (2026, 53:5) applies a statistical filter to remove known temperature influences of ENSO, major volcanic eruptions, and solar variation from the global temperature record. After removal, the residual background warming rate since approximately 2015 is approximately 0.35°C per decade — compared with roughly 0.2°C/decade for the 1970–2015 period — an acceleration with statistical significance exceeding 98%. The authors describe the finding as consistent with but not unexpected from climate models, and flag it as evidence that Paris Agreement 1.5°C targets may be breached before 2030 if the current rate continues. Widely covered by Carbon Brief, The Japan Times, Mongabay, ScienceDaily, and the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research.

arXiv 16 APR
WP-MIP: First Community Intercomparison of AI-Based, Hybrid, and Physics-Based Weather Prediction Models on Common Hindcast Cases

arXiv:2604.16643 · April 2026

The Weather Prediction Model Intercomparison Project (WP-MIP) is a community-coordinated initiative providing the first systematic evaluation of AI-based, hybrid (physics+ML), and purely physics-based numerical weather prediction models against a shared set of hindcast test cases spanning multiple weather regimes and both hemispheres. By enforcing identical initial conditions, observation input, and evaluation metrics across all participating models, WP-MIP addresses the methodological inconsistencies that have made direct performance comparisons across the published literature unreliable. The project represents an analogue to established model intercomparison projects (CMIP, HighResMIP) for the new generation of AI and hybrid NWP systems and is coordinated through an open benchmarking platform.

WP-MIP (arXiv:2604.16643, апрель 2026): первый скоординированный проект межмодельного сравнения ИИ, гибридных и физических моделей прогнозирования погоды на единых гиндкаст-кейсах. Единые начальные условия, наблюдения и метрики устраняют методологическую несопоставимость литературы. Аналог CMIP/HighResMIP для ИИ и гибридных NWP-систем; открытая бенчмарк-платформа.

arXiv: Stretched-Grid vs. Limited-Area Modelling for Data-Driven Regional Weather Forecasting — Architecture Guidance for Sub-Global AI Forecast Systems

arXiv:2507.18378 · July 2026

A preprint posted to arXiv in July 2026 provides a systematic architectural comparison of two approaches to regional AI weather forecasting: (1) limited-area models (LAM), which focus inference over a geographic subdomain cut out from the global grid; and (2) stretched-grid models, which retain global coverage but locally increase resolution over the target region. The paper evaluates both strategies on regional verification metrics and skill scores, offering practical architectural guidance for meteorological services developing sub-global AI NWP systems — a question of direct relevance to European and other regional operational centres considering AIFS-style regional adaptations.

arXiv:2507.18378 (июль 2026): архитектурное сравнение двух подходов к региональному ИИ-прогнозированию — (1) модели ограниченной области (LAM): инференс только над выбранным регионом; (2) «растянутые сетки»: глобальный охват с локально повышенным разрешением. Оценка по региональным верификационным метрикам; практическое руководство для служб с региональными мандатами (релевантно для европейских центров).

WindBorne Systems Raises $37M Series B to Scale AI Weather Intelligence Platform — 600-Balloon Atmospheric Network Feeds WeatherMesh; Valued at $250M

Redwood City-based WindBorne Systems closed a $37 million oversubscribed Series B on August 5, co-led by Khosla Ventures and Galvanize, with TransLink Capital and Lux Capital participating, at a post-money valuation of $250 million — bringing total funding to more than $62 million. WindBorne operates a global network of approximately 600 autonomous long-duration weather balloons launched from 20 sites worldwide; the balloons collect upper-air observations at altitudes and frequencies that polar-orbiting satellites cannot match, feeding the company's proprietary WeatherMesh AI forecasting platform. TechCrunch reported that WeatherMesh outperforms government forecasts on several standard skill metrics at multiple lead times. Proceeds fund atmospheric network expansion and next-generation WeatherMesh model development.

WindBorne Systems (Рэдвуд-Сити, 5 августа): $37 млн Series B, Khosla Ventures + Galvanize (TransLink Capital, Lux Capital), оценка $250 млн; суммарное финансирование >$62 млн. 600 автономных долгоживущих зондов с 20 стартовых площадок — аэрологические наблюдения, недоступные полярно-орбитальным спутникам, → ИИ-платформа WeatherMesh. По данным TechCrunch, WeatherMesh превосходит государственные прогнозы по ряду стандартных метрик. Цели: расширение сети зондов и следующее поколение WeatherMesh.
