China Imposes First-Ever Consumption Tax on Solar Cells and Lithium Batteries to Force Industry Consolidation

China ended a decade-long consumption tax exemption for solar cells and lithium-ion batteries on July 17, imposing a 2% tax on lithium batteries from September 2026 (rising to 4% in September 2027) and a 2% tax on solar cells from April 2027 (rising to 4% in April 2028). The policy is designed to hasten consolidation in China's severely overcrowded solar and battery manufacturing sectors.
