AMS / NOAA 10 AUG
AMS/NOAA "State of the Climate in 2025" Released — CO₂ Record 425.6 ppm, Ocean Heat Content and Sea Level at All-Time Highs, Arctic Second-Lowest Sea Ice, 38th Year of Glacier Loss

The AMS and NOAA's annual State of the Climate report (BAMS, August 10) synthesizes data from 60 countries to confirm 2025 as the warmest year in the 175-year instrumental record. Key findings: atmospheric CO₂ reached a record annual mean of 425.6 ppm, approximately 4 ppm above 2024; global ocean heat content set all-time highs across all measured depth layers (0–700m, 700–2000m, 2000–6000m); global mean sea level rose to a new record; Arctic sea ice area was the second-lowest for the annual minimum; and alpine glaciers recorded their 38th consecutive year of mass loss. Every major global temperature dataset independently confirmed 2025 as the warmest year, providing the peer-reviewed baseline for 2026 climate attribution and adaptation planning.

Ежегодный отчёт АМО/NOAA «Состояние климата в 2025 году» (BAMS, 10 августа) по данным 60 стран: 2025 — самый тёплый год за 175 лет наблюдений. CO₂ — рекорд 425,6 ppm (~4 ppm выше 2024 г.); тепловой контент океана — новый максимум во всех горизонтах (0–700, 700–2000, 2000–6000 м); уровень Мирового океана — рекорд; площадь арктического льда — вторая наименьшая; 38-й подряд год потери массы горных ледников. Все крупные датасеты независимо подтверждают 2025 г.

AP / Las Vegas Sun 05 AUG
This Summer Isn't Breaking Heat Records Globally — Yet. Scientists Warn the Real Records Arrive When El Niño Peaks This Fall

An AP analysis explains why 2026 is currently tracking as the third-warmest year rather than the hottest: the El Niño signal propagates to global-mean atmospheric temperature with a roughly six-to-eight-week lag behind its ocean forcing, meaning the full impact won't register until Q4. Ocean temperatures are already showing the signal: global SSTs have been at or above 2024 record levels nearly every day since June, and 82% of the ocean surface is experiencing marine heatwave conditions per Copernicus Marine.

AP объясняет, почему 2026 г. пока на третьем месте: атмосферный отклик на Эль-Ниньо запаздывает на 6–8 недель. Полный сигнал проявится в Q4. Уже сейчас SST на уровне рекордов 2024 г. или выше — почти каждый день с июня; 82% поверхности океана — в режиме морских тепловых волн.

Why the Spokane Wildfires Became So Catastrophic — Record Snowpack Deficit, Compound Drought, WUI Expansion, and the Heat Dome: A Forensic Analysis

WindBorne Systems Raises $37M Series B — 600+ Autonomous Sensing Balloons, AI WeatherMesh Models; Khosla Ventures and Galvanize Co-Lead; Total Raised $62M at $250M Valuation

05 AUG

WindBorne Systems, which operates a global network of more than 600 long-duration autonomous atmospheric sensing balloons paired with proprietary AI weather models, closed a $37 million Series B on August 5 — the largest dedicated venture round in the AI weather intelligence category this year. The round was co-led by Khosla Ventures and Galvanize, with participation from TransLink Capital and Lux Capital, bringing total funding to more than $62 million and valuing the company at approximately $250 million. WindBorne's WeatherMesh models train on the company's proprietary balloon-derived atmospheric observations, which the company says enables superior skill on severe weather events compared to models trained solely on government reanalysis data. The company tripled both its global balloon constellation and revenue over the past year. Proceeds will expand the balloon fleet, increase GPU compute capacity, and develop the next generation of WeatherMesh forecasting products.
