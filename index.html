Purvah Green Power to Acquire 1.4 GWp Operating Solar Portfolio from ReNew Power for ₹4,859 Crore (~$580M)

Purvah Green Power announced August 11 that it will acquire a 1.4 GWp portfolio of operating solar assets from ReNew Power for ₹4,859 crore (approximately $580M). The portfolio comprises 14 projects across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Karnataka, all commissioned 2020–2024 with PPAs in place. This is a separate transaction from the CESC/Purvah/ReNew new-build financial close ($509M) reported last week. The acquisition will make Purvah the fifth-largest owner of operating solar capacity in India. Closing expected Q4 2026 subject to CERC and CCI approvals.
