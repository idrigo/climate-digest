NOAA / Above The Norm 13 AUG
NOAA Issues First-Ever Extraordinary El Niño Advisory — 90%+ Probability of Very Strong Conditions; 69% Chance of Historic Event Beyond All Records Since 1950; +10°C Subsurface Anomalies

NOAA issued an extraordinary El Niño advisory on August 13 — the first such designation in the agency's history — raising the probability of very strong conditions (Oceanic Niño Index ≥2°C) above 90% for the October–December 2026 peak window, with a 69% chance the event will exceed all records in the 76-year instrumental record since 1950. Subsurface water temperature anomalies in the central and eastern Pacific have reached +10°C — a clear indicator of exceptional thermal energy building in the ocean. The advisory significantly upgrades the risk of record global temperatures in 2026–27, widespread coral bleaching events across the Pacific, and severe La Niña-linked cold anomalies following the event's peak. Major downstream implications include Atlantic hurricane suppression through the end of the 2026 season and amplified drought conditions across southern Africa and South and Southeast Asia.

NOAA (13 августа): первое в истории агентства «чрезвычайное» предупреждение об Эль-Ниньо. Вероятность RONI ≥2°C в октябре–декабре — свыше 90%; шанс превзойти все рекорды с 1950 г. — 69%. Аномалии подповерхностного слоя Тихого океана — +10°C. Ожидаемые последствия: рекордные глобальные температуры 2026–27 гг., массовое обесцвечивание кораллов, подавление ураганов в Атлантике, усиление засух в Южной Азии и Африке.

WMO / Copernicus 13 AUG
WMO and Copernicus Confirm Europe's 2026 as Hottest Summer on Record — Confirmation Extends Beyond June–July; August 2026 Also Hottest on Record

The World Meteorological Organization and Copernicus Climate Change Service jointly confirmed on August 13 that Europe's 2026 summer is now the hottest on record — extending and strengthening the June–July confirmation from August 10. August 2026 is also tracking as the hottest August on record for the continent. The confirmation encompasses both land and sea surface temperature across the meteorological summer (June, July, August). WMO Secretary-General Celeste Saulo described 2026 as the year the 1.5°C Paris Agreement threshold is almost certainly being breached as an annual global mean, with European heat being the most visible regional driver. More than 25,000 heat-related deaths have been recorded across Europe in 2026; the continent has experienced five distinct heatwave events since June.

WMO и Copernicus (13 августа): всё метеорологическое лето 2026 г. (июнь–август) в Европе — самое жаркое в истории. Подтверждение расширено с июня–июля до полного сезона. Август 2026 также устанавливает рекорд. Свыше 25 000 смертей от жары; пять волн зноя с июня. Генеральный секретарь ВМО: 2026 г. практически наверняка станет первым годом, преодолевшим порог 1,5°C Парижского соглашения.
